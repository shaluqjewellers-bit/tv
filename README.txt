SHALUQ TV — INSTALLATION
1. Extract the ZIP, keeping index.html and the assets folder together.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge to preview; press F11 for fullscreen.
3. For Samsung Tizen TV, upload the whole folder to an HTTPS web host, then open its public URL in Samsung Internet.
4. The intro video is included. It is muted to improve browser autoplay reliability.
5. Dubai prayer times use AlAdhan API, city Dubai, method 16, school 0, with localStorage caching.
6. The Saturday market notice appears only on Saturdays (Dubai time).
7. TV browser may suspend pages; test long-running playback and disable TV sleep features as appropriate.
8. Verify prayer timings against your preferred official Dubai timetable before relying on them.
